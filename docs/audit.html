Methodology & Calculation Basis

Compound cost = pack price ÷ (net pack grams × labelled concentration ÷ 100). Rankings and value metrics require matching dated price evidence, verified in-stock observations, and recorded compound concentration.

Nutrition and label metrics are recorded directly from official product packaging; conflicting claims and unverified fields remain highlighted in each offer's evidence breakdown. Unknown values remain unknown and are never imputed with category averages.
