Mathematical Scoring Derivation & Formula Specification

To build absolute trust, every ranking score on this platform is completely transparent and mathematically auditable. We eliminate all subjective reviewer bias and commercial sponsorships.

Protein Bio-Efficiency Score (PBES)

EVALUATION METRIC: 0 — 100+ INDEX
PBES = (100 ÷ Cost_per_g) × (Protein_% ÷ 100) × ((4 × Protein_%) ÷ Energy_100g)
  • 1. Economic Yield (100 ÷ Cost_per_g): Grams of isolated pure protein delivered per ₹100 spent. Combines pack mass, market price, and protein percentage into an objective cost denominator: Cost/g = Price ÷ (Pack_kg × 10 × Protein_%).
  • 2. Protein Purity Ratio (Protein_% ÷ 100): Rewards high-concentration powders (e.g. 85–90% isolates) and penalizes excessive fillers, emulsifiers, or binders.
  • 3. Caloric Lean Efficiency ((4 × Protein_%) ÷ Energy_100g): Pure protein provides 4 kcal per gram. This measures the exact percentage of total calories derived strictly from protein versus non-protein fats, sugars, and maltodextrin.

Creatine Bio-Efficiency Score (CBES)

EVALUATION METRIC: 0 — 100+ INDEX
CBES = (100 ÷ Compound_Cost) × (Creatine_% ÷ 100) × Caloric_Cleanliness
  • 1. Active Compound Yield (100 ÷ Compound_Cost): Grams of verified active creatine compound delivered per ₹100 spent: Compound_Cost = Price ÷ (Pack_g × Creatine_% ÷ 100).
  • 2. Compound Purity Ratio (Creatine_% ÷ 100): Evaluates active compound purity (100% for pure Monohydrate/HCl; proportionally adjusted for flavoured formulas or blends with HMB/Taurine).
  • 3. Caloric Cleanliness: 1 - min(0.5, Energy_100g ÷ 400). Pure zero-calorie creatine receives a multiplier of 1.0; blends diluted with carbohydrates or sugars receive proportional penalties.
Verification Guarantee: No brand can buy, sponsor, or artificially inflate a placement in these rankings. All calculations are executed strictly on verified official price and nutrition label data snapshot as of 25 October 2026.
