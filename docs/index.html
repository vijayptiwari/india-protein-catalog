Research Methodology & Standards

How we evaluate, verify, and calculate every supplement offer in the catalog.

Actual Single Packs Only

We research actual single-pack selling prices. We never extrapolate prices from a 1 kg pack to assume what a 2 kg pack costs, nor do we scale bulk discounts down. Accessory combos and multipacks are excluded.

Active Nutrient Cost Denominators

Pricing uses actual disclosed nutrient mass rather than total powder mass. Flavoured blends are never assumed to have unflavoured 100% purity. Missing values remain null, never zero.

Chemical Form & Trademark Integrity

Monohydrate, HCl, and Nitrate are distinct chemical forms. Creapure® and Creasure® are ingredient trademarks, not separate compounds. Costs use the labelled compound, not estimated free-creatine equivalents.

Auditable Evidence Links

Every record includes direct links to official store snapshots and nutrition labels. Discrepancies between FAQs, packaging photos, and serving claims are explicitly documented in evidence notes.
