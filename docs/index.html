Laboratory Verification Protocol

Rigorous, standardized scientific guidelines applied to eliminate commercial marketing bias.

1. ISO-Pack Integrity (Zero Extrapolation)

Empirical analysis relies strictly on real single-pack selling points. We never algorithmically extrapolate a 1 kg price to estimate 2 kg or 4 kg tiers. Multi-packs and accessory bundles are quarantined.

2. Active Compound Denominator Assays

Pricing formulas evaluate actual disclosed nutrient concentration, not inert powder mass. Flavoured products never inherit 100% pure concentration without explicit lab evidence. Unknown metrics remain null.

3. Biochemical Form & Trademark Rigor

Monohydrate, HCl, and Nitrate are distinct chemical entities. Creapure® and Creasure® are patented trademarks, not chemical variants. Compound costs retain hydrate and salt counter-ion portions.

4. Auditable Source Dossiers

Every individual specimen links directly to official archived manufacturer product dossiers and nutrition tables. Serving conflicts, packaging anomalies, and label discrepancies are fully documented.
