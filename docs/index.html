4 Common Traps to Watch Out For When Buying in India

Marketing tricks used by supplement companies that cost Indian buyers thousands of rupees every year:

🔍

1. The Giant Scoop Trick

A brand advertises "30g protein per serving!", but when you read the label, the scoop is 48g! That means the powder is only 62% protein, padded with thickeners and carbs. Always check Protein %, not the headline scoop number.

🍬

2. The Flavoured Creatine Markup

Companies sell 100g of flavored creatine for ₹1,200 by adding cheap dextrose sugar and fruit flavoring. In contrast, 250g of 100% pure unflavoured monohydrate costs around ₹800. Don't pay 2x more for sugar.

🎁

3. The "Free Shaker" Combo Markup

Brands charge ₹400–₹600 extra on e-commerce combos by bundling a cheap ₹50 plastic shaker bottle or a low-grade t-shirt. Always buy standalone 1 kg or 2 kg refill packs to save money.

🧪

4. Maltodextrin & Gum Bloat

Check the ingredient list: if maltodextrin, corn syrup solids, or modified starch appear near the top, you are paying whey prices for cheap carbohydrates that cause bloating and insulin spikes.

How We Score Every Product: Transparent Math, Zero Bias

We do not accept brand sponsorships. Here is the exact calculation used to evaluate every product:

Protein Value Score

FOR WHEY, PLANT & CASEIN
Value Score = (100 ÷ Cost per g) × (Protein % ÷ 100) × (Protein Calories ÷ Total Calories)
  • 1. Cost per Gram of Pure Protein (100 ÷ Cost/g): How much pure protein you get for ₹100. Formula: Pack Price ÷ (Pack kg × 10 × Protein %). For example, a 1 kg pack of 80% whey at ₹2,199 delivers 800g pure protein = ₹2.75 / g (~₹66 per 24g scoop).
  • 2. Protein Purity Ratio (Protein % ÷ 100): Rewards 75%–90% concentration per scoop, and penalizes powders loaded with thickeners or excessive flavorings.
  • 3. Lean Calorie Ratio: Pure protein is 4 kcal per gram. We check what fraction of total calories comes directly from protein versus unwanted fats and added carbs.

Creatine Value Score

FOR MONOHYDRATE, CREAPURE® & HCL
Value Score = (100 ÷ Cost per g) × (Creatine % ÷ 100) × Cleanliness Multiplier
  • 1. Cost per Gram of Active Compound (100 ÷ Cost/g): Shows exactly what you pay for active creatine, not filler weight.
  • 2. Compound Purity %: 100% pure unflavoured monohydrate or Creapure® gets full marks (1.0). Flavored powders with only 60–70% creatine are penalized.
  • 3. Zero-Sugar Cleanliness: Creatine should provide explosive strength without unwanted calories. If a powder adds 300 kcal of dextrose sugar per 100g, its score is discounted.
