How We Score: The Math Behind Every Ranking

We believe in 100% transparency. No black-box algorithms, no secret influencer deals. Here is the exact, honest formula used to rank every product on this platform:

Protein Value Score

FOR WHEY, PLANT & CASEIN
Value Score = (100 ÷ Cost per g) × (Protein % ÷ 100) × (Protein Calories ÷ Total Calories)
  • 1. Cost per Gram of Pure Protein (100 ÷ Cost/g): We calculate how much pure protein you get for ₹100. Formula: Cost/g = Pack Price ÷ (Pack kg × 10 × Protein %). For example, if a 1 kg pack of 80% whey costs ₹2,199, you are paying ₹2.75 per gram of protein (~₹66 per 24g scoop).
  • 2. Protein Purity Ratio (Protein % ÷ 100): We reward brands offering 75%–90% real protein per scoop, and penalize powders stuffed with maltodextrin, thickeners, gums, or excessive flavorings.
  • 3. Lean Calorie Ratio: Pure protein provides 4 kcal per gram. We calculate what fraction of total calories comes directly from protein versus unwanted fats and added carbs.

Creatine Value Score

FOR MONOHYDRATE, CREAPURE® & HCL
Value Score = (100 ÷ Cost per g) × (Creatine % ÷ 100) × Cleanliness Multiplier
  • 1. Cost per Gram of Active Compound (100 ÷ Cost/g): Formula: Cost/g = Pack Price ÷ (Pack Grams × Creatine % ÷ 100). Shows exactly what you pay for active creatine, not filler weight.
  • 2. Compound Purity %: 100% pure unflavoured monohydrate or Creapure® gets full points (1.0). Flavored powders with only 60–70% creatine are penalized.
  • 3. Zero-Sugar Cleanliness: Creatine should provide explosive strength without unwanted calories. If a powder adds 300 kcal of dextrose sugar per 100g, its score is discounted.
