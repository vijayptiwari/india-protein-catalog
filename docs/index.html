How these comparisons work

Compound cost = pack price ÷ (net pack grams × labelled concentration ÷ 100). Rankings show the best eligible pack per brand and product, ordered by grams of labelled compound per ₹100. Eligible offers need a matching dated price observation, known concentration and in-stock status.

Nutrition is reported from product pages and labels; shared label profiles and conflicts remain visible in each offer’s evidence. Unknown values remain unknown. Creatine forms are compared using their stated compound weights, without assuming chemical equivalence.
