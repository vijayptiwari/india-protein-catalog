Laboratory Calculation Standards

Dual-Yield Denominators. Bulk Powder Cost = Pack price ÷ Pack grams. Active Compound Cost = Pack price ÷ (Pack grams × Labelled compound % ÷ 100). A 3 g reference dose comparison cost is computed in Details without clinical endorsement.

ISO-Pack Standard. All verified single-pack weights are retained across pouches and canisters. Multi-pack promos, free shaker bundles, gummies, and compressed tablets are strictly excluded.

Chemical Form Integrity. Monohydrate, hydrochloride (HCl), and nitrate represent distinct molecular entities. Creapure® and Creasure® are patented manufacturing trademarks. Unflavoured purity is never extrapolated to flavoured formulas.
