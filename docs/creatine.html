Two cost measures. Powder cost = price ÷ pack grams. Compound cost = price ÷ (pack grams × labelled compound percentage ÷ 100). Unknown concentration means no compound cost. A 3 g comparison quantity is available in Details and filters; it is not dosage advice.

Actual packs only. All verified single-pack weights are included, including small jars and pouches. No extrapolated 1 kg, 2 kg or 4 kg prices. Bundles, multipacks, free gifts, tablets and gummies are excluded. Sold-out offers are labelled.

Forms and evidence. Monohydrate, hydrochloride and nitrate are chemical forms. Micronization describes processing. Creapure and Creasure are trademarks.
